Fill the company created from a VBSell CNPJ with the Brazilian registry.

The lookup uses the provider already configured in Brazilian
Localization CNPJ Search. It stays off until a company enables it. A
registry error does not block the opportunity.

Configuration
=============

Install Brazilian Localization CNPJ Search and choose the provider
(ReceitaWS or SERPRO) in the Brazilian localization settings. ReceitaWS
allows three lookups per minute. SERPRO is a paid service.

Then, in the VBSell block of CRM settings, enable Look Up CNPJ for each
company that should query the registry. Companies that leave it off keep
the name sent by VBSell.

Usage
=====

When the option is on, a VBSell contact with a CNPJ still creates the
company and the linked person. Odoo then asks the configured CNPJ
service for the official name, address and fiscal data and writes them
on the company. The opportunity customer remains the person.

A company that already has a legal name is not queried again.
Shareholders returned by SERPRO are attached under the company and do
not replace the VBSell person. If the service times out or rejects the
CNPJ, the opportunity is created with the data VBSell sent.
